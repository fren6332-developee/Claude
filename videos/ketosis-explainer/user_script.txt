Ever wonder how your body can go from hangry to a full-on fat-burning furnace? That's ketosis, and no, it's not magic — it's just chemistry showing off. Eat fewer carbs, and your liver's sugar stash — glycogen — empties out faster than free snacks at a meeting. That drop makes insulin, the hormone whose whole job is "save everything," chill out and drop too. Low insulin is like a bouncer stepping aside — fat cells fling the doors open and dump stored fat into your blood. Your liver scoops up all that fat and cooks it into ketones — basically premium fuel for your brain and muscles, no sugar required. Less carbs, less insulin, shrinking fat cells — that combo right there is the actual weight-loss plot twist. Now toss in a workout. Your muscles start pumping out lactate like it's getting paid overtime. That lactate zooms straight to your brain and cranks up BDNF — a protein that basically waters your brain cells so they grow stronger connections. Real science, real fuel, one seriously overachieving body.
